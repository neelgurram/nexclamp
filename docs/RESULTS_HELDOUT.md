# Held-out confirmatory results (campaign `heldout-v1`)

*Confirmatory evaluation preregistered at AsPredicted (#312455, 2026/09/19 17:29 PT). Every number
below is read from sealed outputs: the primary endpoint from
`results/processed/heldout-v1/heldout_evaluation.json` (frozen pipeline, commit `c9230ae5`), the
rest from `results/tables/heldout-v1/secondary_summary.json` (`scripts/heldout_secondary.py`).
Never pooled with Pilot 1 or Pilot 2. Written 2026-09-22; not yet checked by the human audit.*

## 1. What was run

- **Models:** 6 held-out models from 4 sources never used in development: `hay2011_soma`,
  `bbp2015_soma`, `traub2005_testseg2`, `traub2005_testseg_all`, `smith2013_singlecomp`,
  `migliore2005_ca1_soma`.
- **Variants:** 186 generated by the frozen rules: 120 primary semantic mutants, 18 numerical
  stress tests, 48 behaviour-preserving controls. The registration estimated "roughly 100-150";
  the exact number is reported and was fixed by the frozen generator, not chosen.
- **Strategies:** canonical regression test (each model's shipped simulation) versus the battery
  selected on development models (`P05_long_step`, `P04_step_2x`, `P06_ramp`). Selection was
  allowed up to 4 protocols (k = 4); the greedy rule stopped at 3.

## 3. Primary endpoint

| Strategy | Detected | Rate | 95 % cluster CI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canonical | 69 / 82 | 0.841 | 0.78 - 0.91 |
| Selected battery | 80 / 82 | 0.976 | 0.92 - 1.00 |

- **Δ = +0.134**, 95 % cluster-bootstrap CI **[+0.013, +0.221]** (B = 10,000, seed 20260917,
  6 clusters).
- Paired counts: both 67, battery only 13, canonical only 2, neither 0.
- Exact McNemar p = 0.007 (treats mutants as independent). Cluster permutation p = 0.156
  (exact, 64 sign assignments; smallest attainable p = 0.031).
- Per model (battery minus canonical): bbp2015 +2, hay2011 +2, migliore2005 +3, smith2013 +4,
  traub2005_testseg2 +2, traub2005_testseg_all **-2**.

**Against the preregistered criteria**

- The point estimate exceeds the material difference of 0.10; the interval excludes 0 but its
  lower end is below 0.10, so a material advantage is **supported by the estimate, not
  established by the interval**.
- Canonical regression is **not adequate** by the preregistered rule (≥ 0.95): 0.841 on
  features, 0.936 with its full trace counted as well.
- Inconclusive rules, none triggered: control false positives 0 / 48; survivors failing h/4
  confirmation 0 / 18; tolerance entries refinement-excluded 0 %.
- Branch rules (fixed before Pilot 2): **A, hidden drift supported**: 6 confirmed full-trace
  canonical survivors on 3 models (hay2011_soma 4, bbp2015_soma 1, migliore2005_ca1_soma 1).
  Level C was calibrated on every held-out model and fired on 11-17 faults per model, so these
  are not the X-26 artefact. B (feature-level insufficiency) is also met.

## 4. Secondary endpoints

**S1 Random batteries.** All 56 random 3-protocol batteries: mean 0.846, 2.5-97.5 % range
0.53-0.96; the selected battery ranks at the 99th percentile, share at least as good 0.018.
10,000 random batteries within the same cost: mean 0.909, range 0.84-0.98; share at least as good
0.030. Selection on development models transferred to unseen models.

**S2 Silent survival** (class 6 / (class 5 + class 6)): 13 / 82 = 0.159, cluster CI 0.089-0.222.
No mutant was detected by the canonical protocol at h only.

**S3 False positives on valid transformations:** 0 / 48 for every strategy (exact 95 % upper bound
0.074), including at half tolerance.

**S4 By family and severity**

| Group | n | Canonical | Battery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kinetics (held-out family, never used in selection) | 22 | 0.864 | 0.955 |
| Families seen in development | 60 | 0.833 | 0.983 |
| Maximal conductance | 17 | 0.647 | 1.000 |
| Gating time constant | 5 | 0.400 | 1.000 |
| Mild faults | 40 | 0.700 | 0.950 |
| Strong faults | 31 | 0.968 | 1.000 |

For the unseen kinetics family the difference is +0.091 with cluster CI [-0.10, +0.22], flagged
unreliable (few informative clusters): **no conclusion about the unseen family**. For the seen
families it is +0.150, CI [+0.036, +0.232]. The canonical test's losses are concentrated in mild
faults.

**S5 Coverage.** `P05_long_step` alone reaches 90 % of exhaustive coverage; the three selected
protocols reach 95 %. 100 % is not reached without the canonical protocol, because 2 faults on
`traub2005_testseg_all` are detected only by it.

**S6 Cost.** Per variant the battery costs 1.0 M cell-steps against 0.31 M for canonical (3.3x);
per detected fault 1.03 M against 0.36 M.

## 5. Canonical features versus canonical full trace (never merged)

On the 94 faults with any detected change: features 69, full trace 87, features only 1, trace only
19, either 88, **neither 6**. Most of what the canonical feature check misses, its own full trace
catches; the 6 it misses on both are the Branch A survivors, all caught by the battery through
spike count or spike timing, reproducible at h, h/2 and h/4.

## 7. Limitations

Six models, so intervals are wide and the model-level permutation test is underpowered; one
simulator (jNeuroML/jLEMS); admissibility depends on the definition (82 versus 94); 186 variants
against a planned 100-150; a 3-protocol battery against a "k = 4 battery" in the registration;
the human audit is pending; the result for the unseen kinetics family is inconclusive.
